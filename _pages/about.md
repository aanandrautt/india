I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, where I focus on developing intelligent systems for structural safety under extreme conditions[web:23][web:25].

## Research Overview

My research centers on developing **machine learning-based early warning systems** for predicting fire-induced structural collapse in reinforced concrete buildings[web:25][web:29]. I combine advanced computational methods with structural engineering principles to create predictive frameworks that can save lives by providing critical advance warning before catastrophic failure[web:23].

My work addresses a critical gap at the intersection of structural fire safety, artificial intelligence, and wildland-urban interface (WUI) hazards[web:25]. Using sophisticated finite element modeling in ANSYS and ABAQUS, coupled with PyTorch-based deep learning architectures, I develop probabilistic models that can forecast structural response under fire loading with unprecedented accuracy[web:29].

## Current Projects

I am currently working on:

- **AI-driven collapse prediction**: Developing recurrent neural network architectures for time-series forecasting of structural displacement and failure progression during fire events[web:25]
- **Fragility analysis**: Conducting probabilistic assessments of concrete buildings exposed to wildfire-driven ember attacks and simultaneous internal-external fire scenarios[web:23]
- **Change point detection**: Implementing algorithms to identify critical transition points in structural behavior that signal impending collapse[web:25]
- **Early warning framework**: Creating operational thresholds and safe evacuation time calculations based on real-time structural health monitoring data[web:29]

## Technical Expertise

## Research Impact

I am preparing manuscripts for submission to premier journals including *Computers and Structures*, focusing on translating complex computational models into actionable safety protocols[web:29]. My work aims to bridge the gap between academic research and practical implementation in building codes and emergency response systems[web:25].

Beyond my primary research, I am passionate about advancing the application of artificial intelligence in civil infrastructure, particularly in scenarios where human safety depends on accurate, real-time predictions of structural behavior under extreme environmental conditions[web:23][web:25].
