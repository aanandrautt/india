I am a PhD candidate (2021–present) in the Department of Civil and Infrastructure Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, India. My research focuses on fire safety of buildings, where I develop engineering models to investigate structural behavior under fire and provide design recommendations. I also develop AI-based models to forecast structural responses during fire events using sensor and optical measurement data. These models are designed to monitor structural health in real-time, support decision-making at fire rescue sites, and enhance safety by providing early warnings of potential failures.

My journey in civil engineering began at the National Institute of Technology Patna, where I completed my Bachelor's degree (2017–2021) and developed a strong foundation in structural mechanics while cultivating an interest in artificial intelligence, which motivated me to pursue a PhD in this field. Today, my work spans structural mechanics, nonlinear analysis, structural fire engineering, and AI applications in structural engineering. I am particularly passionate about leveraging the predictive power of machine learning to transform how we design, monitor, and maintain critical infrastructure.

## Current Projects

I am currently working on:

- **AI-driven collapse prediction**: Developing recurrent neural network architectures for time-series forecasting of structural displacement and failure progression during fire events
- **Fragility analysis**: Conducting probabilistic assessments of concrete buildings exposed to wildfire-driven ember attacks and simultaneous internal-external fire scenarios
- **Early warning framework**: Creating operational thresholds and safe evacuation time calculations based on real-time structural health monitoring data

## Technical Expertise

- **Computational Fluid Dynamics**: ANSYS FLUENT, OpenFOAM

## Research Impact

I am preparing manuscripts for submission to premier journals including *Computers and Structures*, focusing on translating complex computational models into actionable safety protocols. My work aims to bridge the gap between academic research and practical implementation in building codes and emergency response systems.

Beyond my primary research, I am passionate about advancing the application of artificial intelligence in civil infrastructure, particularly in scenarios where human safety depends on accurate, real-time predictions of structural behavior under extreme environmental conditions.
